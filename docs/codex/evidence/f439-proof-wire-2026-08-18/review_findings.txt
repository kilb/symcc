F439 multi-round review findings
================================

Round 1 - CLI, environment, and documentation contract
Finding: the oracle examples used --lidrup-checker, but the official-oracle CLI
accepts --lidrup-check. The service flag remains --qfbv-lidrup-checker.
Fix: corrected docs/Testing.txt and benchmark/README.md and verified both help
contracts independently.

Round 2 - wire semantics and integer boundaries
Finding: the official PalRUP tracer/converter negates signed inputs while
encoding them, so INT_MIN/LONG_MIN are not portable values. The implementation
already used the symmetric [-MAX, MAX] literal range and positive MAX IDs, but
the rejection boundary was not explicit in tests.
Fix: added INT_MIN and LONG_MAX+1 rejection assertions; retained exact official
converter comparison, canonical-varint, truncation, overflow, and unknown-tag
negative cases.

Round 3 - external checker process and identity boundary
Finding: reviewed executable replacement, output-pipe deadlock, unbounded
stdout/stderr, timeout, marker/version/mode, and receipt-replay risks.
Result: the checker executes a private content snapshot, binds its SHA-256,
drains bounded output concurrently, enforces timeout, and validates receipts.
No additional defect remained after the existing mutation tests.

Round 4 - immutable CAS, concurrency, and commit authorization
Finding: reviewed create-or-verify publication, quota races, staging leftovers,
artifact/receipt substitution, and the backend-to-QueryStore trust handoff.
Result: concurrent identical publication converges, conflicting content and
quota overflow fail closed, and QueryStore reruns external verification before
commit. Existing concurrency and mutation tests cover these cases.

Round 5 - evidence closure and research claim boundary
Finding: the implementation needed full-suite evidence and an explicit split
between PalRUP fragment interoperability and global confirmation.
Fix: sealed the 1320-test Python gate, serial LLVM 17/18 gates, official-tool
oracle, source manifest, diagrams, five reviews, and machine-readable claim
boundary. local_check/redistribute/confirm remains deliberately outside F439.
