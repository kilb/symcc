F430 reuses an ephemeral, warmed Z3 memory image on one Linux host by forking a
single-thread parent. It is not a portable serialized solver state, a cross-node
heap transfer protocol, or a guarantee that Z3 reuses every learned clause.

The oracle compares mechanism cost against same-version cold Z3 processes for a
fixed one-byte prefix. The 2.252x and 2.249x ratios do not establish end-to-end
symbolic-execution throughput, fuzzing coverage, time-to-target, or bug-finding
uplift. Those claims require repeated paired public-target campaigns.

SAT remains subject to backend and QueryStore Query IR validation. UNSAT remains
unknown unless the separately configured authorization policy accepts it. A
target timeout preserves the parent snapshot, but portfolio cancellation kills
the process group and discards it.
