Audited algorithm:
Paper Algorithm 2 and artifact parasuit/value_sample.py. The paper explores
when silhouette <= threshold; the implementation exploits when silhouette >=
threshold. F397 follows the official implementation at equality. F397 uses a
deterministic dependency-free bounded MeanShift adaptation, normalized value
axis, scheduler reward and worker-cost correction; it does not claim sklearn
decision or random-sequence equivalence.

Upstream results are not local results:
The paper evaluates KLEE 2.1 on 12 GNU C programs for 24 hours with five
repetitions. Its reported 25.8% branch-coverage advantage over Symtuner, 11
unique bugs (four exclusive), and value-stage ablation gains belong only to
that study. F397 performs no public-target campaign and does not reuse those
numbers as SymCC results.
