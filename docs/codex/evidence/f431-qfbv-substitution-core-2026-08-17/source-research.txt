Interpretation: F431 migrates structural variable-substitution core matching
into the existing proof-carrying QF_BV and QueryStore authority chain. Its
current variable domain is 8-bit input reads. It does not claim to reproduce
all SMT sorts, evaluation targets, or measured reuse rates from Cache-a-lot.
