F421 research positioning

LoopSCC: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02863
  General motivation for compositional reasoning about complex loop SCCs.

Automatic Partial Loop Summarization in Dynamic Test Generation:
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/automatic-partial-loop-summarization-in-dynamic-test-generation/
  Prior partial-summary direction based on guards and induction relations.

LLVM Language Reference: https://llvm.org/docs/LangRef.html
  Authoritative icmp, select, poison and integer wrap semantics.

Local claim boundary:
  F421 is a finite, two-level, affine-address and affine-leaf Decision DAG
  proof language. It is not a general LoopSCC implementation and does not yet
  replace loop execution. Executable replacement is staged separately under
  the F421 refinement contract as F422/F423.
