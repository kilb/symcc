legacy hint manager and kept the model outside the execution/correctness root.

R7 - Reproducibility audit: bound provider/model/prompt/transport identities;
command identities include executable and wrapper/script content digests.

execution, added a separate one-shot selection event, and left actual execution
to the later outcome record.

duplicate local edge reports now receive authoritative deltas 1 then 0.

R10 - Trigger audit: added persistent reactive gating for solver barriers,
symbolic zero-yield and global-coverage plateaus; productive results suppress
model calls. Online/shadow/fallback share the same trigger policy.

R11 - Identity audit: made fallback decision IDs iteration-bound and unique;
bound hint selections and outcomes to source/candidate digests and routed
candidate outcomes back to the originating episode.

R12 - Resource/input audit: bounded command and HTTP output streams to 1 MiB
before parsing, killed timed-out command process groups, added overflow tests,
and visually revised the schematic to remove misleading action dependencies.

Residual boundary: the deterministic command oracle tests mechanisms, not LLM
quality or public-target coverage. Source-level slicing and long-duration
online/shadow/fallback experiments remain R-track work.
