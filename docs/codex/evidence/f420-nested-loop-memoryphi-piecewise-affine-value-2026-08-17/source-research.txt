F420 primary-source positioning, 2026-08-17

LLVM Language Reference:
  https://llvm.org/docs/LangRef.html
  Relevance: exact icmp/select semantics and nuw/nsw poison behavior.
  Boundary: F420 accepts only six equality/unsigned scalar predicates and
  ordinary modular add/mul; it is not a general LLVM expression normalizer.

Automatic Partial Loop Summarization in Dynamic Test Generation:
  https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/automatic-partial-loop-summarization-in-dynamic-test-generation/
  Relevance: derives useful partial summaries from loop guards and induction.
  Boundary: F420 uses a statically finite two-level instance domain and does
  not reproduce SAGE's dynamic hypothesis-and-validation algorithm.

LoopSCC:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02863
  Relevance: inside-out composition of summaries for complex loops.
  Boundary: F420 handles one direct select at a writer, not general SCC,
  oscillatory-interval, or multi-branch loop interpretation.

Verified Multi-Abstraction Summaries:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09550
  Relevance: separates summary generation from validation.
  Boundary: F420 is bounded structural reconstruction with an independent
  consumer, not a reproduction of the complete formally verified system.
